Ségolène Martin


Email Github Scholar CV



I am a tenured researcher (Chargée de Recherche) at Inria Lyon in the OCKHAM team.
My current research is at the intersection of optimization and generative learning, with applications to image processing and computer vision.
Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at TU Berlin, in the team headed by Prof. Gabriele Steidl. I completed my PhD in applied mathematics at CentraleSupélec, Université Paris-Saclay, in the CVN / OPIS Inria team. I did my PhD under the supervision of Prof. Jean-Christophe Pesquet (CVN, CentraleSupélec) and Prof. Ismail Ben Ayed (ILLS, ETS Montréal).

Research interests


  • Generative modeling
    Flow-based methods (mainly) for imaging
  • Inverse problems for imaging
    Image restoration and reconstruction in the Bayesian framework
  • Classification
    Clustering and few-shot optimization-based methods, transductive learning,
    text-vision models
  • Large-scale continuous optimization
    Proximal algorithms, subspace acceleration, convergence theory
